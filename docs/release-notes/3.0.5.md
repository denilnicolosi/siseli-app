# Smart Inverter App v3.0.5

## Equipment status
- The inverter circle now shows **Inverter** and its own consumption (losses) inside it, like the other nodes. The separate "Losses" chip below the diagram is gone; tap the inverter for the explanation.
- The inverter is never highlighted in red: its consumption is normal.

## Layout
- Tighter side and top margins on the Equipment status and Energy history cards, the same as the forecast card. The history chart is wider.
- Smaller refresh and fullscreen buttons in the card headers.
- Chart series buttons fit on one row; "Production" is now "Solar".

- App version: 3.0.5+18
- MSIX identity version: 3.0.5.18
