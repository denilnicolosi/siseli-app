# Smart Inverter App v3.0.3

## Battery readings
- **More realistic battery power.** The cloud sends battery current in whole amps, rounded down (a night draw of ~1.5 A reads 0–2 A, i.e. 0–106 W). Below 3 A, with nothing on the AC input, battery power is now estimated as solar − consumption − inverter self-consumption; with a full battery it reads 0.
- New setting **Inverter self-consumption** in Settings → Station parameters (default 60 W: the Easun SMH III 6.2 kW manual declares 50 W at no load, night data gives ~58 W).
- Default battery capacity is now 100 Ah.
- History no longer drops to 0 on empty records (about 1 in 10 from the cloud); they are skipped in the charts and in the day/week/month totals.

## Charts and forecast
- Day view with a single series selected: the **peak** is marked with a dot and a "Peak W · HH:mm" line (for the battery, also the deepest discharge).
- Each solar forecast day shows a **weather icon** (sun, sun and cloud, cloud, fog, drizzle, rain, snow, thunderstorm); tap it for the name.
- Fixed: with only Battery selected, the history section could go blank after 23:00.

## Equipment status
- Tapping a node shows only **live readings** (voltages, currents, states, temperature); settings and ratings stay in the Data tab.

## Updates
- Tapping the "update available" notification downloads and installs the new version. The notification is no longer repeated at every check.
